The Origins

Born from a passion for unparalleled culinary excellence, RIORA is Hubballi's premier luxury cloud kitchen. We believe that dessert is not merely an afterthought, but the grand finale of a dining experience.

Sourcing only the finest European cocoa, artisanal dairy, and rare ingredients, our creations are meticulously crafted to deliver a multi-sensory journey. Every cake that leaves our kitchen is a testament to the art of fine baking.

Chef's Signatures

Bestseller

The Dream Cake

A mesmerizing five-layer chocolate indulgence. Featuring a rich mud cake base, layered with French chocolate mousse, ganache, and enrobed in a mirror glaze. A true masterpiece.

Classic

Premium Truffle

Dense, dark, and utterly decadent. Made with 70% dark single-origin chocolate, this truffle cake is a sophisticated balance of bitterness and deep cocoa flavors.

Signature Fusion

Rasmalai Fusion

An elegant marriage of Indian heritage and European pastry. Cardamom-infused sponge layers soaked in saffron milk, frosted with delicate pistachio mascarpone.
